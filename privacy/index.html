PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy, simply.

Effective date: August 12, 2026

Overview

Fyzzle is designed to work on your Mac. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Information stored on your Mac

Fyzzle stores your preferences locally, including mascot choices, reminder schedules, focus-timer state, launch-at-login preference, and mascot position. This information remains on your device.

Permissions

Fyzzle may request notification permission to deliver optional reminders. It may request Accessibility permission for its optional meeting-awareness feature, which checks for supported meeting-app activity so reminders can be postponed. Fyzzle does not record your screen, meetings, keystrokes, browser content, or audio.

Analytics and tracking

Fyzzle does not include third-party advertising, tracking, analytics, or accounts.

Children

Fyzzle does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
